Mana Bay

The challenge
A premium waterpark wasn’t reaching the right audience, and its ticket price needed explaining.
What I did
Led the creative and digital growth sprint, helping establish the creative department, content system and digital acquisition engine.

My part

The case study

Overview

A six-month creative and digital growth engagement with Mana Bay waterpark.

Objective

Reach the right audience, and make the case for the price: one ticket for the whole park, built to international standard.

My role

As Head of Digital, I set the strategy and led creative direction for a four-person team covering video, graphic design and digital marketing. We ran Meta campaigns, an organic influencer programme built on reels (including “This is Mana Bay”) and the Mana Turns 1 anniversary iPhone giveaway.

Outcome

Average daily website visitors grew from 12 to roughly 2,200 over six months in 2024, and park guests from 2 to 800+. The traffic came from Meta campaigns, influencer reels and the Mana Turns 1 giveaway.

The case study

Overview

Banglalink’s Toffee streamed the FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022 in Bangladesh.

My role

As Toffee’s Content Manager, I led the UGC side: the creator journey, the go-to-market plan, and Toffee Tiki Taka Talk, which ran through the tournament.

Toffee reported more than 25 million viewers across the platform by the Round of 16 (VEON press release). That’s a platform-wide figure; my part was the creator programme and Toffee Tiki Taka Talk.
