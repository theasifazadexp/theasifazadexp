Questions

How is this different from a consultant or an agency?

A consultant hands over a report and leaves. An agency delivers outputs. A strategic partner is in the room for the decisions and still there when the plan meets reality. Your own team or an agency can do the work, and I keep it connected.

What does a month look like?

A weekly or fortnightly working session, access between sessions for questions and reviews of campaigns or creative, and a monthly review of numbers and priorities. You can see the work list at any time.

What’s the minimum commitment?

We agree the term in the written scope. Decisions take a few cycles to show their effect, so a partnership usually runs for several months.

Can you work alongside my existing team or agency?

Yes. That’s what the role is for. I brief the people doing the execution, review what they produce and keep it pointed at your goals. I don’t replace people who are doing their job well.

Who is this not right for?

Founders who want someone to produce posts and nothing else, and anyone who asks for advice but won’t make decisions. Both are better served by a production team, or by deciding first.

What happens when we stop?

You keep the documented priorities, plans and processes so your team can carry on. If you want to work together again later, we pick up from where we left off.

Not sure this fits? Talk it through before you decide anything.
