What it is

One idea → many assets → many platforms → many opportunities

Every piece gets a job: attention, trust, authority, demand or action. A flagship piece carries your best thinking, a repurposing map turns it into everything else, and a shared workspace shows who is doing what and when.

The aim is steady, useful output that doesn’t depend on one person having a good week.

Who it’s for

Best for: Teams creating content post by post.

  • You publish regularly but each piece starts from a blank page.
  • Content depends on one person, and stalls when they’re busy.
  • You can’t say which pieces bring in enquiries.

Probably not the right fit if:

  • You need a single campaign or a one-off video.
  • You only need an editor or a production crew.

Questions

What is a flagship (mothership) piece?

One main piece, such as a long video, a podcast episode or an essay, that carries your best thinking. Clips, posts, emails and quotes are cut from it. One strong idea then does the work of many small ones.

Do you produce the content, or just design the system?

Either. I can set the system up and train your team to run it, or my team can run parts of it with you. The decision is how much production you want to keep in-house.

How many people do I need on my side?

At least one person who owns the system and one who can edit or produce. If you have neither yet, the first job is deciding who they’ll be.

What tools will we use?

Whatever you already pay for, where possible. The plan lives in a shared workspace you can open at any time to see what’s planned, in progress and published. We run our own work the same way.

How do you keep quality up when output goes up?

Every piece has a brief and a review step before it’s published. Each month we look at the numbers and cut what isn’t working. More output isn’t the aim. The right output is.

Is this only for video?

No. The same logic works for video, audio, writing and social posts. Choose the flagship format your audience will actually give time to, then build around it.

Not sure this fits? Talk it through before you decide anything.
