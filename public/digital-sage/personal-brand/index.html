Questions

Do I need to be on camera?

No. The format should fit what you’ll keep doing: writing, audio, video or a mix. Video builds trust quickly, but a founder who writes a sharp note every week beats one who films twice and stops.

I’m not well known. Is a personal brand worth building?

Often, yes. You don’t need a large audience. You need a clear position and proof, shown to the right people. We start from what you’ve already done and what you know that others in your market don’t say.

How much of my personal life do I have to share?

As much as you choose, and nothing more. We set the boundary in the first month and write it down, so anyone posting on your behalf knows where it is.

Will you write as me?

We can draft and edit in your voice, but the ideas and opinions have to be yours. If you wouldn’t say it out loud, it doesn’t go out. That’s what keeps a personal brand believable.

Should I use Bangla, English or both?

Your audience decides. Many founders in Bangladesh speak to two audiences at once: customers at home, and partners or investors who look them up in English. We set the language for each platform in the plan.

How long before I see results?

A clear position takes weeks. Audience trust takes months, and I won’t promise follower numbers. In the first 90 days you should have sharper messaging, a steady publishing habit and a way to track which pieces start conversations.
