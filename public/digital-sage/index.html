  3. We use what we sellOur own projects run on the same streamlined, productivity-first approach we set up for clients.
  4. Less tiring to work withWe designed the process to avoid the long back-and-forth most clients expect from agencies. Progress is visible, so you aren’t left asking.

Questions

How much does it cost?

I don’t publish prices because every engagement is scoped to your situation, team and goals. After a discovery call you receive a written scope and price before anything starts.

Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?

Yes. I’m based in Dhaka and my team is spread across Bangladesh, the UK and Canada, so we work with clients anywhere. Calls are scheduled around your time zone.

Who will actually do the work?

I lead every engagement and bring a team of specialists behind me. Who does what is agreed in the scope, so you know exactly who is working on your project.

Which service should I choose?

If your online presence is messy, start with Digital Strategy. If you are the asset, choose Personal Brand. If you publish a lot but it feels chaotic, choose Content Systems. If you need senior judgment on an ongoing basis, choose Strategic Partner. Not sure? Book a call and we’ll work it out.

How do I get started?

Book a discovery call. We talk through your situation. If we’re a fit, you receive a written scope. If we’re not, I’ll say so and point you somewhere better.
